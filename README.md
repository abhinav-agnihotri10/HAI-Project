HAI Project - 
COMPAS (Correctional Offender Management Profiling for Alternative Sanctions) 
The COMPAS dataset contains Criminal Offender Profiles and Risk scores compiled by ProPublica in 2016 from Broward County, Florida, USA, 
for criminal justice risk assessments by mainly utilizing the target variable of two_year_recidivism 
i.e., data of re-offending within 2 years of release from prison.
